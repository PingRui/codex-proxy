# NEXORA Third-Party Notices

NEXORA includes third-party dependencies. Those components remain subject to
their own copyright notices and licences; the NEXORA non-commercial licence
does not replace or restrict those separate terms.

Dependency identities, versions, integrity records, and available licence
metadata remain recorded in the repository's lockfiles and in dependency
metadata distributed with installed or packaged components, including:

- [`package-lock.json`](./package-lock.json)
- [`web/package-lock.json`](./web/package-lock.json)
- [`native/package-lock.json`](./native/package-lock.json)
- [`native/Cargo.lock`](./native/Cargo.lock)

This document is a concise notice, not a replacement for the applicable
third-party licence texts or metadata. It intentionally does not duplicate the
thousands of dependency entries already preserved in those sources. When
redistributing NEXORA, retain the dependency notices and licence materials
included with the source and packaged dependencies.
